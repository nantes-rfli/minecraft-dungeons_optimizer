# Melee/Ranged 30 Independent Identity Source Discovery Audit

## Scope, target reconciliation, and baseline

This audit checks only the current needs-additional-identity-evidence Melee/Ranged set. It does not create aliases or Formal Identity assertions and does not populate any of these 30 items.

The 30 IDs were reacquired from the current repository's needs-additional partition in docs/remaining_formal_identity_breadth_readiness_audit.md, then checked against the current melee and ranged catalogs, Formal Identity, the Structural Inventory manifest, every candidate shard, and canonical source records. The reconciliation is exact: 16 of 82 Melee items and 14 of 71 Ranged items; all 30 are outside Formal Identity. Every candidate below is provisional, has one retained candidate, exactly one CDO, and decoded ItemIdName and ItemId.SerializedId values that agree. Candidate role is instance for 29 records and storable for shivering-bow.

The requested baseline 5f75dd32b88f8ee3cd8887a3d06c968ff4a1356b was fetched and verified as current origin/main. Its tree includes the audited Corrupted Seeds identity state from 8b7b0c0 and contains 144 Formal identities. The source inventories, aliases, Structural shards, and candidate sets below were checked against this baseline tree.

## Decision rules and source coverage

A Raw value is an observation, not proof of canonical identity. The Structural seeds for all 30 use provisional search results, with presentation hints recorded as non-identity evidence; 26 also record variant-family/event search. The generator source in scripts/game-data-raw-structural-inventory.ts confirms those are candidate-search mechanisms. Candidate uniqueness, serialized-catalog-identifier-match, names, package basenames, icon paths, family, suffixes, and variant relations are not counted as independent bridges.

For every target, I checked the canonical identity record in research/source-harvest.json and the category audit; research/domain/game-data-entity-aliases.json; Formal Identity; scripts/equipment-icons.ts ENTITY_OVERRIDES; scripts/game-data-model-alignment.ts; scripts/audit-attack-event-comparison.ts; generated/equipment-icons/manifest.json as presentation/search hint only; Raw Structural CDOs, imports, inheritance chains, and outgoing reference edges in the target shards; Raw Asset Index and parsed package coverage for DataTable/catalog leads; and committed mapping/research history. None of these sources provides a current accepted/high canonical-domain-to-Raw-ID bridge for any target. 28 matching raw-ID alias rows are unresolved; pride-of-the-piglins and shivering-bow have no matching alias row. No target has a canonical ENTITY_OVERRIDES mapping.

The canonical source locator in each row identifies the committed identity claim and its source IDs. The Raw locator points to the candidate CDO in the exact shard. Current source records often include display names, gameplay/mechanics facts, or family relations, but do not assert the Raw ItemIdName or SerializedId.
