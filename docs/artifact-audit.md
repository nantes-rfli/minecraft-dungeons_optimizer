## Source reconciliation

- Corrupted Seeds: Wiki exposes CorruptedSeeds ID and 1.3.2.0/1.16.2.0 history; absent from the primary list. [Evidence](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dungeons:Corrupted_Seeds).
- Corrupted Pumpkin: database pack says Howling Peaks and labels Common; event history identifies a seasonal unique Beacon variant. Raw reported pack is retained separately; do not infer DLC purchase requirement.
- Database properties and editorial prose have different provenance. Many Wiki live pages were robots-blocked; search-index copies supplied concrete stats/history. No game-file Power coefficient dump or current high-Power heal curve was obtained.
- Harvester CD 8/4s and Lightning Rod CD 4/2s remain unresolved; the preferred database value is recorded, not registered.
