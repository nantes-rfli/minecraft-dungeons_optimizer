## First batch

- **Cool Down**: reuse Artifact activation/cooldown state; preserve external, intrinsic, enchant, and future ranged ownership. Reported -40% intrinsic and enchant reductions multiply rather than add; copy semantics still require a bounded policy.
- **Potion Barrier**: reuse PotionState and Defense, but gate Armor DR / Iron Hide / Guarding Strike / Weakening / Protection composition.
- **Chilling**: reuse the Control axis; model a two-second pulse and one-second slow as periodic coverage, never as direct scalar Defense.

The other mandatory deep-audit entries are Cowardice, Snowball, Protection, Deflect, Thorns, Health Synergy, Final Shout, Bag of Souls, Soul Focus, Reckless, and Death Barter.
